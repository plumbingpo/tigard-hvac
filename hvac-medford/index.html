A little caution while you wait for us can prevent a much larger repair. A frozen air conditioner should be shut off at the thermostat with the fan set to "on", which lets the coil thaw safely. A furnace that keeps short-cycling, or any burning smell, means it's time to shut the unit off at its service switch and call us immediately.

Keeping Your Medford HVAC System Running Longer

A little routine care makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that breaks down in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so small issues get caught early.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners last 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet the same part, ignored for a season, can take out the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts now so you can reach us fast the moment something sounds or feels wrong in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
